EVAN'S TOP 5 ANIME FLIMS

1. Spirited Away

* A young girl named Chihiro enters a mysterious spirit world and must work in a magical bathhouse to save her parents, who have been transformed into pigs.
* Why it's special: Stunning world-building, memorable characters, and a beautiful coming-of-age story.

2. Your Name

* Two teenagers living in different parts of Japan suddenly begin swapping bodies. As they grow closer, they discover a mystery that connects their fates.
* Why it's special: Emotional storytelling, breathtaking animation, and an unforgettable soundtrack.

3. A Silent Voice

* A former bully tries to make amends with a deaf girl he mistreated during elementary school.
* Why it's special: A powerful story about redemption, friendship, guilt, and forgiveness.

4. Princess Mononoke

* A young warrior becomes caught in a conflict between humans expanding their civilization and the spirits protecting the forest.
* Why it's special: Epic action, complex themes, and one of the greatest environmental stories in animation.

5. I Want to Eat Your Pancreas

* A quiet boy discovers that a cheerful classmate is suffering from a terminal illness, and their friendship changes both of their lives.
* Why it's special: Heartfelt characters and an emotional story that stays with many viewers long after it ends.
